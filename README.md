# Grid Defender

An 8-bit, turn-based classroom game that teaches cybersecurity and critical infrastructure using an epidemic model (SEIR). Students defend the fictional city of Riverton's power, water, hospital, telecom, banking and transit systems for 60 days.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or play the hosted version on GitHub Pages (see below). No installation, accounts or internet connection are needed apart from loading the fonts.

## What students learn

- **SEIR modeling:** systems move from Vulnerable (S) to Exposed (E, hidden intruder) to Compromised (I) to Secured (R).
- **R₀ = β·k̄/γ:** how spread rate (β), network connectivity (k̄) and recovery rate (γ) decide whether an attack fizzles or explodes.
- **Cascading failures:** when power fails, dependent sectors go dark without being hacked.
- **Phishing awareness:** students spot the fake email among three real-looking ones and learn the red flags.
- **Defense in depth:** patching, segmentation, monitoring, backups, staff training and multi-factor login.

## How to play

1. Select a system on the map.
2. Spend budget on Patch, Restore, Scan or Isolate, and buy upgrades.
3. Press **End Day** (or `N`) to see what attackers do overnight.
4. Handle incident alerts and phishing inbox challenges as they appear.
5. Review the end-of-shift report and discuss the questions.

Keyboard: `P` patch, `R` restore, `S` scan, `I` isolate, `N` end day, `1`–`3` report an email.

## Privacy

The game stores no data and sends nothing to a server.

## Notes

All organizations, people and email addresses in the game are fictional.
